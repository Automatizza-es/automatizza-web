## Prompt final

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: background photograph for an interactive automation product demonstration, wide landscape 16:10.
Primary request: a quiet premium editorial photograph of a neutral office desktop with a modest stack of real invoices and business documents in the lower left foreground.
Scene/backdrop: a clean pale warm-gray stone or matte desk surface; softly blurred light neutral office background, no busy decor. Natural diffuse daylight. Subtle realistic paper edges and soft contact shadows.
Composition/framing: wide landscape, camera close to desk level but slightly elevated. The stack occupies the lower left quarter, extending toward the bottom edge. Keep the upper half and the entire right half calm and nearly empty, a very light greige backdrop for crisp HTML interface elements that will be added later. Stack should be visible in the foreground, with 5–10 slightly offset sheets, natural asymmetry and believable thickness, the top invoice with tiny understated printed gray rows, a small heading FACTURA, no readable personal data.
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial product photography, refined and minimal, high key, soft contrast. White, warm gray, subtle beige, restrained dark ink. Fine tactile paper detail, understated desk texture.
Constraints: photograph only. NO floating file cards, NO interface, NO loader, NO data card, NO arrows, NO connection lines, NO digital graphics, NO blue glow, NO large writing, NO people, NO logos, NO watermarks. Avoid laptop or office clutter. Leave plenty of useful negative space for web UI overlays. Image should feel calm and natural, not a 3D illustration.
