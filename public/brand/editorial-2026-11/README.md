# Current logo assets

The three SVG files without `-web` are the unmodified files supplied by the owner.
The `-web.svg` variants retain their text, geometry and colours, crop export margins
and replace opaque white cutout shapes with transparent SVG masks. This lets both
wordmarks and the blue symbol work on their intended backgrounds.

The PNG wordmark is used by the site's existing logo structured data. Browser,
Apple and manifest icons use the supplied blue symbol. Previous logos remain at
their original public/brand paths; previous raster icons are archived under
public/brand/archive/pre-2026-11.
